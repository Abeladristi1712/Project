03

Adhi Green Building

Adhi Green Building is a conceptual office project at the Simpang Lima intersection in Semarang. Designed for a prominent urban setting, it proposes a contemporary workplace that integrates green-building principles with a recognisable corporate presence.

The façade layers vertical fins and horizontal shading elements to reduce direct solar exposure. Planted terraces, green walls, and landscaped foreground bring vegetation through the building and strengthen its relationship with the public realm.

The project positions shading, greenery, daylight access, and outdoor landscape as integral to the identity of a greener urban workplace.

2024 / STUDIO PROJECT
SEMARANG, INDONESIA

Profile

Building performance, sustainability, and data-driven design.

I am an Architectural Engineering graduate with professional experience in green building certification, building performance analysis, sustainable design, and infrastructure development across both government and private-sector projects. My experience includes conducting building performance assessments, supporting green building certification processes, performing building envelope analyses such as OTTV, RTTV, and WWR calculations, and contributing to large-scale public infrastructure projects through project monitoring, integrated data management, and interactive dashboard development. I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Building Performance and Sustainability at the National University of Singapore (NUS), where I am further developing my expertise in building energy simulation, indoor environmental quality, computational fluid dynamics, sustainable building design, and data-driven approaches for high-performance buildings. My professional interests focus on integrating architectural design, building science, sustainability, and digital technologies to support the development of energy-efficient, low-carbon, and high-performance built environments.
